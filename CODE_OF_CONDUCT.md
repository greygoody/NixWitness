# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in NixWitness a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We aim to build a community where technical disagreement is direct, evidence-based, and compatible with treating people decently.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- showing empathy and respect toward other people;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and behavior;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and confidentiality;
- documenting technical disagreements so others can understand the decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- deliberately introducing secrets, private data, or harmful code;
- retaliating against someone who reports a concern;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting channel for this repository and mark the report as a Code of Conduct concern. If that channel is unavailable, open a minimal public issue requesting private contact without including sensitive details.

Project maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the privacy and safety of reporters where possible, and may remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should recuse themselves when another suitable reviewer is available.
